Welcome to London Fields Cricket Club

A community cricket club based in Hackney, North East London. We're members of the North East London Cricket League and play friendlies across the summer throughout the borough.

Always on the lookout for new talent — if you're interested in any form of cricket, get in touch via the club email on our About page.
